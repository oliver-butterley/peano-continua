# Adversarial reviews of 2026-11-05

Record of the independent reviews made while the formalisation was extended to the whole paper.
Each reviewer was a separate agent that had not seen the author's assessment, worked read-only, and
was asked for counterexamples, source fidelity and design problems. Findings were then applied;
this file records the verdicts and the main findings, not every editorial point.

## 1. New cited axioms, first batch

Axioms: `exists_homotopyEquiv_finiteGraph`, `eda_exists_map_earring`, `exists_nhds_monodromy`.

- **Verdict: all three OK**: true, implied by the sources read literally, no stronger than them.
- Finite graphs: arXiv:2510.22901 Thm 3.9 (deformation retract onto a finite graph when
  `π₁ ≠ 1`) and Cor 3.3 with the contractibility of dendrites (when `π₁ = 1`). Degenerate cases
  checked: the point (`W = univ`, no arcs) and a circle (one vertex, one arc).
- Eda's Theorem 6.1: checked against the restatements in Kent arXiv:1305.4215 (Thm A, Thm 2.1),
  which are at least as strong; Eda's own text not inspected. The concatenation and base-change
  directions match the paper's `φ_α`.
- Discrete monodromy: Brazas–Fischer Prop 9.13 + Def 9.2 + Remark 9.4(a) + Lemma 9.5 with `H = 1`,
  base point `x`, `α` constant; this instantiation is used verbatim in their Lemma 9.17.
- Applied: audit prints and `axioms.md` rows were missing; added.

## 2. New cited axioms, second batch, and the Part A definitions

Axioms: `nonempty_homeomorph_loopWildSet` (restated with the loop wild set),
`eda_homotopyEquiv_of_homeomorph`, `coveringDimLE_one_of_interior_eq_empty`,
`exists_reduced_homotopy`, `isReparam_of_reduced_homotopic`, `eda_zeroForm`,
`exists_simpleClosedCurve`. Definitions: `affineI`, `subpathMap`, `IsReducedPath`, `IsReparam`,
`IsZeroFormCut`.

- **Verdict: all OK.**
- Brazas–Fischer's wild set (points where `X` is not semilocally simply connected, l.1234) is
  exactly the complement description of `loopWildSet` for locally path-connected spaces.
- Eda 2022: hypotheses from the abstract (zero-dimensional and **non-empty** wild part); only the
  direction "homeomorphic ⇒ homotopy equivalent" assumed.
- Dimension: `CoveringDimLE` is the Čech–Lebesgue covering dimension; the axiom is Hurewicz–Wallman
  IV.3 + V.8 and holds even without compactness.
- Reduced paths: matches Constantine–Lafont Def 2.8 / Thm 2.10. Paths that pause are not reduced,
  so reparametrisation by homeomorphisms is the right notion.
- 0-forms: the cut encoding (`m + 1` pieces, fixed-point-free involution, so an even number `≥ 2`)
  is equivalent to the paper's Definition 3.1 with the convention of `A.md`, A-3.1-1.
- Residual risk: Eda 2016 (Lemma 3.6) and Eda 2022 were not inspected beyond abstracts.
